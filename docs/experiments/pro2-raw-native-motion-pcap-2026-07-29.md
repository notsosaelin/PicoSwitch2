# Genuine Pro Controller 2 raw/native motion PCAP audit — 2026-07-29

## Question

Does the genuine Pro Controller 2's 40-byte handle-`0x000E` motion PDU contain raw
ICM-42670-P FIFO packets, a separate magnetometer/reference quaternion, or Nintendo's packed
multi-sample IMU representation?

## Result 1: handle `0x000A` is a trustworthy raw-IMU reference

All 1,766 report-`0x05` notifications are 63 bytes. The 18-byte field at report offsets
`0x2A..0x3B` decodes little-endian as:

| Offset | Size | Field |
|---:|---:|---|
| `0x00` | 4 | microsecond timestamp |
| `0x04` | 2 | signed temperature |
| `0x06` | 6 | signed accel X/Y/Z |
| `0x0C` | 6 | signed gyro X/Y/Z |

Observed checks:

- timestamp elapsed / PCAP wall elapsed = `0.991642`;
- median timestamp step = `22,500 µs`, matching the BLE notification interval;
- mean temperature = `10.842` raw = `25.085 °C` using the ICM conversion;
- median non-startup acceleration norm = `4,238.85` counts, close to the documented
  `4,096 counts/g` scale while the controller is moving.

One directly testable sample:

```text
timestamp = 0x0002ABB7
temperature = 10
accel = (-89, 589, 4149)
gyro  = (-18, 3, 20)
```

This is the correct ground-truth observation point for future raw-to-native correlation work.

## Result 2: the 40-byte PDU is not native ICM FIFO

The exact ICM-42670-P tables define:

- Packet 3: 16 bytes, header high nibble `0x6` when accel+gyro are present;
- Packet 4: 20 bytes, header high nibble `0x7` when accel+gyro+20-bit extension are present.

Across 2,275 genuine 40-byte PDUs:

- only `10/2275` happened to have Packet-4-looking high nibbles at offsets 0 and 20;
- Packet-3-looking pairs occurred at sparse, inconsistent offsets (`0`: 4, `4`: 15,
  `5`: 1, `6`: 4, `7`: 1, `8`: 3);
- PDU byte 0's high nibble was approximately uniform because it is the changing timing word.

Those are chance matches, not a stable FIFO framing. This upgrades the older entropy-based FIFO
rejection: the exact component datasheet has now been consulted and tested directly.

The ICM datasheet also contains no quaternion, sensor-fusion, or magnetometer output format. Any
fused carrier is generated by the controller MCU, not emitted natively by this IMU.

## Result 3: the Pro2 capture fits the packed multi-sample catch-up map

The PDU begins with the already known 12-bit 800 Hz tick plus a 12-bit elapsed-count field and a
one-byte layout/status discriminator. The remaining 36 bytes are a 288-bit LSB-first payload.
Successive genuine Pro2
reports span mostly 17–19 internal ticks:

```text
17 ticks: 903 reports
18 ticks: 788 reports
19 ticks: 530 reports
```

Because the true delta is at least 15, 2,274 classified records use the catch-up layout. The
initial reference-only map was corrected by the later same-pose cadence matrix:

| Payload bits | Field | Encoding |
|---:|---|---|
| `68..109` | accel 1 XYZ | 3 × signed 14-bit, 4096 counts/g |
| `110..157` | gyro 1 XYZ | 3 × signed 16-bit, divide by 4 for ordinary gyro counts |
| `158..196` | accel 2 XYZ | 3 × signed 13-bit, multiply by 2 for ordinary accel counts |
| `197..244` | gyro 2 XYZ | 3 × signed 16-bit, divide by 4 for ordinary gyro counts |
| `245..286` | accel 3 XYZ | 3 × signed 14-bit, 4096 counts/g |

Independent plausibility checks on this Pro2 capture:

- accel 1 median norm = `1.027 g`;
- accel 3 median norm = `1.027 g`;
- accel 1 versus accel 3 axis correlations = `0.9947`, `0.9953`, `0.9881`;
- payload bit 0 was set in `2275/2275` reports;
- high-rate payload bit 1 was set in `2273/2275` reports.

The two full-resolution acceleration samples tracking one another at the documented scale and exact
bit positions first established the packed-sample family. The later cadence matrix resolved the
middle acceleration and both gyro fields without requiring impossible simultaneous CCC delivery.

## Correction: G6/G7/G8 are not independent magnetic lanes

The historical `G6/G7/G8` codec selected these inclusive payload bit ranges:

```text
G6: 204..225
G7: 228..249
G8: 252..271
```

In the catch-up map:

- gyro 2 occupies bits `197..244`;
- accel 3 occupies bits `245..286`.

Therefore:

- G6 crosses gyro-2 X/Y;
- G7 crosses gyro-2 Y/Z and accel-3 X;
- G8 crosses accel-3 X/Y.

The nearly constant derived norm and apparently stable “second quaternion” were artifacts of
cutting across real packed gyro and gravity-bearing acceleration samples, not evidence of a
separate vector. This also explains why the controlled external-magnet campaign produced no
repeatable response.

The 2026-07-29 magnet experiment remains valuable negative evidence: it correctly established
that those aliases did not respond to magnetic polarity or distance. Its former semantic label is
what changes.

## Untapped control-path result

The `0x000A` capture proves a genuine Pro Controller 2 can emit the raw report-`0x05` IMU path.
Its initialization sequence differs from the `0x000E` session:

```text
common sequence:
  0A/02 payload 0x00000003
  0C/02 payload 0x0000002F
  calibration reads
  0C/04 payload 0x0000002F

0x000A session:
  subscribe CCC handle 0x000B

0x000E session additionally:
  07/01, 16/01, 11/03, 0A/08
  subscribe CCC handle 0x000F
```

Every displayed command response in both reference sessions reports the normal `0x1078` success
marker. The prior live `magraw` positive control was aimed at feature bit 7 and did not reproduce
this report-selection distinction. Its negative result remains true for that tested sequence but
does not mean PID `0x2069` is incapable of handle-`0x000A` raw IMU reporting.

The default-off `imuref` path now replays the reference `0x0A/0x02` report selector, feature mask
`0x2F`, first three calibration reads, report-rate write, and handle-`0x000A` CCC. `imuref off`
restores the validated native `0x27`/handle-`0x000E` production profile.

Success means `imuref status` becomes active, its `common` notification count advances, and the
analyzer decodes nonzero timestamp/temperature/accel/gyro values. The diagnostic also counts any
remaining native notifications. If both handles remain live, a later on-device paired ring can
align them directly. If they are mutually exclusive, repeat one deterministic movement script in
each mode and compare timing/scales without claiming sample-perfect alignment.

### Symmetric CCC handoff

Report selection requires an explicit handoff; layering a cold-session raw control sequence over an
already-active native CCC does not work (see the corrections table). The gate is symmetric:

1. `imuref on`: disable native CCC `0x000F`, require ATT success, then run the raw profile and
   enable common CCC `0x000B`;
2. `imuref off`: disable common CCC `0x000B`, then run the validated native profile and enable
   native CCC `0x000F`.

The ATT result is exposed as `imuref status.att`. Failure to disable native is fail-closed: the
raw transition is abandoned while the still-active production stream remains untouched.

### Hardware result

The explicit CCC handoff was then flashed and tested against a genuine Pro Controller 2
(`057E:2069`) while the adapter remained attached to a real Switch 2:

```json
{"requested":true,"active":true,"transition":false,"att":"0x00",
 "common":259,"native":1}
```

The one native notification was already in flight during the transition. The native count then
stopped while the common count advanced beyond 2,800. A zero-drop 540 ms UART capture retained
72/72 active handle-`0x000A` samples:

- sensor time / capture wall time = `0.990932`;
- median sensor step = `7,500 us`;
- mean temperature = `25.033 C`;
- stationary acceleration norm median = `4,309.63` counts;
- stationary raw means: accel `(79.7, -708.6, 4250.3)`, gyro
  `(-0.71, -1.47, 1.42)`.

`imuref off` returned ATT success and immediately restored fresh, owned native length-`0x28`
motion. This hardware-validates the raw selector, exclusive notification ownership, decoder, and
reversible production restore.

A same-pose, zero-drop production capture retained 53 length-`0x1E` and 21 length-`0x28`
notifications. It first exposed an analyzer error: comparing adjacent `0x28` ticks folds
intervening `0x1E` time into a false catch-up delta. Comparing the immediately preceding native
carrier showed every retained `0x28` had a seven-tick delta and therefore the high-rate form. The
later offline semantics pass below found the packet's complete encoded elapsed field, which is now
the authoritative self-contained classifier; the predecessor remains an integrity check.

### Simultaneous-CCC discriminator

`imuref dual on|off` is a second, default-off UART gate. It is accepted only
after `imuref on` has established the raw profile. `dual on` enables the native `0x000E` CCC
without changing the verified raw report selector; diagnostics expose the ATT result and both
notification counters.

Hardware rejected simultaneous delivery. Enabling the native CCC returned ATT `0x00`, stopped the
common counter at `387`, and advanced the native counter beyond `2,900`; a zero-drop capture
contained 46/46 handle-`0x000E` records and no handle-`0x000A` record. Disabling only the native
CCC resumed the common counter without replaying the raw command profile. Report generation is
therefore selector-exclusive, with native CCC priority, but CCC-only A/B switching is reversible.

`imuref dual off` removes the extra native subscription while retaining the raw profile.
`imuref off` always clears dual-probe state and restores the production native profile.

A zero-drop raw -> native -> raw capture then retained 46 raw and 21 native records. The raw
microsecond timestamp and native 12-bit 800 Hz tick are both monotonic, but the transition has a
path-dependent offset of roughly 23--29 sensor ticks rather than sample-identical epochs. This
prevents naive direct pairing but still permits tightly bracketed stationary comparisons.

The production 7.5 ms link's `0x28` records span seven ticks. They are a distinct high-rate class,
not the normal form and not the catch-up form. Applying normal offsets to these packets produced
impossible stationary vectors, which correctly established a format distinction but did not mean
the packet was opaque.

apparently corrupt 15-tick packets under one decoder. Re-decoding those five 15-tick records as
catch-up produced three stationary acceleration norms of approximately `1.052 g`.

### Complete cadence-dependent field maps

The 36-byte body after timer/elapsed/status is treated as a 288-bit LSB-first payload.

| Layout | Payload bit map | Scale back to ordinary ICM counts |
|---|---|---|
| high-rate, `0..10` ticks | flags `0..1`; continuous vector 3 × signed24 at `2..73`; accel 1 `74..139` signed22; gyro `140..205` signed22; accel 2 `206..271` signed22; tail `272..287` | divide the continuous vector by 4 to compare with other layouts; divide IMU vectors by 256 |
| normal, `11..14` ticks | flags `0..1`; continuous vector 3 × signed22 at `2..67`; accel 1 `68..109` signed14; gyro 1 `110..148` signed13; accel 2 `149..187` signed13; gyro 2 `188..229` signed14; accel 3 `230..271` signed14; tail `272..287` | multiply middle gyro/accel by 2; others unchanged |
| catch-up, `15+` ticks | flags `0..1`; continuous vector 3 × signed22 at `2..67`; accel 1 `68..109` signed14; gyro 1 `110..157` signed16; accel 2 `158..196` signed13; gyro 2 `197..244` signed16; accel 3 `245..286` signed14; reserved-zero pad bit `287` | divide both gyros by 4; multiply middle accel by 2; other accel unchanged |

The high-rate map was found by searching the production packets for the raw A/B/A stationary
vector, then checking the only exact tiling: `74 + 66 + 66 + 66 + 16 = 288` bits. In the zero-drop
raw/native/raw capture:

- raw accel median was `(79, -709, 4250)` counts;
- high-rate accel 1 median was `(80.24, -707.24, 4248.75)` counts;
- high-rate accel 2 median was `(78.66, -708.32, 4249.67)` counts;
- raw gyro median was `(-1, -2, 2)` counts;
- high-rate gyro median was `(-0.70, -1.63, 1.94)` counts.

The older moving `sw2_native_passthrough_live_2026-07-21.jsonl` capture provides an independent
dynamic check. Its 69 classified high-rate packets span gyro norms up to `2688.7` ordinary counts
while both acceleration lanes remain near gravity. Accel 1 versus accel 2 correlations are
`0.9957`, `0.9905`, and `0.9824` by axis. The map therefore tracks real controller motion rather
than merely fitting one stationary bit pattern.

At 15 ms, all three normal acceleration lanes had median norm approximately `1.052 g`; their
axis values agreed with the bracketed raw stream after applying the one-bit resolution correction.
At 18.75–22.5 ms, all three corrected catch-up lanes likewise had median norm approximately
`1.052 g`. Both catch-up gyros agree with the raw stationary bias after division by four.

This hardware-validates every accel/gyro lane, axis order, signed width, scale, and cadence branch
in the length-`0x28` payload.

The cadence transitions also constrain the prefix structurally. Every classified matrix record uses
packing mode `3`. At tick 10→11 transitions, dividing the high-rate carrier by four produces a
continuous trajectory into the normal form, and normal→catch-up retains the same offsets exactly —
one continuous three-component carrier across all three layouts, differing only in fractional bits.

### Extended cadence bound

The bounded sweep was extended through the largest accepted diagnostic interval, 30 ms. All 144
classified records at 25, 27.5, and 30 ms retained the catch-up layout, and catch-up bit 287

### Preamble and layout discriminator

The packet classifies itself. The normalized analyzer accepts both `blecap` and direct
`motionpair` JSONL, preserves the entire interleaved length-`0x1E`/`0x28` sequence, and unwraps the
12-bit sensor timer:

```text
elapsed_ticks = (pdu[2] << 4) | (pdu[1] >> 4)
sensor/layout status = pdu[3]
```

Across 44 zero-drop genuine-controller files:

- encoded elapsed matched the immediately preceding carrier's tick delta in `1274/1274`
  comparable records;
- status `0x0D` selected high-rate in `865/865` records;
- status `0x0E` selected normal in `158/158` records;
- status `0x0F` selected catch-up in `269/269` records.

Classification is therefore self-contained: the first `0x28` in a capture is classifiable, and a
dropped UART record does not force the next packet into a guessed layout. In the long reference
PCAP, encoded elapsed agreed with the observed predecessor in `2263/2274` comparisons; the eleven
disagreements identify missing capture predecessors while the encoded field and `0x0F` status
continue to select coherent catch-up packets. The predecessor comparison remains useful as an
integrity check.

### Prefix carrier

The prefix is a mode-3, cadence-dependent truncated projection of the length-`0x1E` orientation
carrier, sampled at:

```text
prefix epoch = current tick - encoded elapsed + 4
             = preceding carrier tick + 4
```

Field layout, fixed scales, corpus counts, and the fit methodology are in
[`pro2-mode3-carrier-prefix-2026-07-29.md`](pro2-mode3-carrier-prefix-2026-07-29.md). The chart-state
law and the prefix seam behavior at every chart boundary are in
[`pro2-carrier-chart-transition-2026-07-29.md`](pro2-carrier-chart-transition-2026-07-29.md). Those
two documents are authoritative for the prefix; this one is authoritative for the IMU payload,
the raw-handle reference, and the cadence matrix.

A causal decoder that chooses the nearest modular windows from the preceding length-`0x1E` chart
reproduces the interpolated reference with median angular errors `0.000968°` and `0.004682°`, with
no chart mismatch in 92 dynamic records. Exact integer projection and rounding remain open, and no
length-`0x28` generator follows from a diagnostic fit.

### Tail temperature

The high-rate/normal 16-bit tail is two adjacent Q3 IMU-temperature samples sharing a signed
ten-bit integer part:

```text
integer = sign_extend_10(tail >> 6)
temperature_a_q3 = integer * 8 + tail[2:0]
temperature_b_q3 = integer * 8 + tail[5:3]
temperature_c    = 25 + temperature_q3 / 1024
```

Two zero-drop raw/native/raw A/B/A captures bracket this independently:

- `pro2-imuref-raw-native-raw-2026-07-29.jsonl`: raw mean `4.357`, native mean `4.28125`,
  raw mean `4.167`;
- `pro2-imuref-15ms-raw-native-raw-2026-07-29.jsonl`: raw `4`, native mean `3.951923`, raw `4`.

Native ranges of `4.0..4.625` and `3.25..4.0` expose the fractional resolution the integer raw path
discards. The two low triplets are equal in `993/1023` zero-drop records because adjacent samples
usually share a fractional eighth; the unequal 3% are real sample variation, not padding.

Catch-up replaces this tail with the single residual bit 287, which stayed zero in all 1,066
catch-up records across 14 repository captures. The established catch-up fields occupy exactly 287
payload bits, so bit 287 is observed byte-alignment padding. A future firmware escalation could
assign it meaning; no captured condition does.

## Corrections made during this investigation

Recorded so they are not re-derived. Each was a real measurement that led to a wrong label, and
each was corrected within this campaign.

| Wrong interpretation | What it actually was | How it was caught |
|---|---|---|
| `G6/G7/G8` are a magnetic/reference vector | Bit ranges crossing packed gyro-2 and accel-3 samples | Exact bit-range comparison against the decoded catch-up map |
| The 40-byte PDU is raw ICM-42670-P FIFO | Nintendo's own packed multi-sample format | Datasheet Packet 3/4 nibble test: only `10/2275` chance matches |
| Prefix is `flags2` plus three equal-width lanes | `mode2` plus `s24+s23+s25` / `s22+s21+s23` | Cadence widths differing by exactly two fractional bits |
| The two bits after carrier 1 are a separate state field | Carrier 2's low two bits, contiguous LSB-first | Attempting to derive the state's transition law |
| The prefix uses a constant tick offset | `preceding carrier tick + 4`, from the encoded elapsed field | A mixed-cadence capture that no single constant could fit |
| Naive smallest-three at signed-width/`sqrt(2)` is the decode | Refuted as an exact model | 16 genuine records exceed unit retained norm, max `1.026738` |
| Adjacent `0x28` ticks give the catch-up delta | Intervening `0x1E` time folds in and fakes a delta | Comparing against the immediately preceding native carrier |
| A cold-session raw control sequence can layer over an active native report | Report generation is selector-exclusive | `common:0, native:170` after the first live attempt |

The magnet campaign in
[`pro2-magnetic-stimulus-matrix-2026-07-29.md`](pro2-magnetic-stimulus-matrix-2026-07-29.md)
remains valid negative evidence for its tested conditions. Only its semantic label changed.
